Message from Faculty Coordinator

A quick note from leadership on where we're headed and how we support our members.

"I wanted to take a moment to thank our outgoing members and welcome new members to what promises to be another exciting year for the Information Technology Student Organization Community (ITSOC). Our field is one of constant innovation and problem-solving, and the society is here to support you every step of the way."

We'll be hosting a variety of events this year, including guest lectures from industry professionals, workshops to hone your practical skills, and hackathons to connect with your peers. These events are designed to complement your academic studies perfectly.

Dr. Neeta Singh

Head, Department of Information Technology Department
